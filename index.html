How it works

Join

Lock one contribution as a security deposit. Your position is the round you get paid.

Contribute

Pay in each round. Every member's payment writes a different ledger entry, so a whole circle can pay in the same ledger.

Someone misses

The shortfall comes out of their deposit, automatically. Whoever's turn it is still gets paid in full, on time.

Finish

After N rounds everyone has paid N times and received once. Take your deposit back.

If a member misses a round and their deposit is already spent, the contract does not invent money - the pot is short, and it says so. Reporting that honestly matters more than pretending to guarantee something a contract cannot.

Reads are simulated against a public RPC node and cost nothing - no wallet, no account, no fee. Writes are transactions your own wallet signs. There is no CircleFi server and no custody: the contract holds the funds and the rules, and anyone can settle a round.
